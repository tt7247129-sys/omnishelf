OmniShelf reads your books. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no trackers, and nothing locked behind an upgrade.

WHAT IT OPENS
34 file types: PDF, XPS, EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, AZW4, FB2, PDB, PRC, DJVU, the comic archives CBZ and CBR, DOCX (Android 8+), DOC, ODT, RTF, Markdown, TXT, HTML, XHTML, MHT, JSON and ZIP.

READING
- Day, night, sepia and fully custom themes
- Fine control of font, margins, line spacing and alignment
- Single page, two page and scrolling modes
- Text-to-speech using your device's built-in voice, works offline
- Highlights, notes and bookmarks with quick navigation
- Continue reading: a card on the shelf takes you back to the last page

LIBRARY
- Five shelves: recent, library, folders, favourites, bookmarks
- Grid, compact, list and cover layouts; sort by date, title, author, size
- Instant search; pin a book to the top; long-press a cover to pin
- OPDS catalogues for public and self-hosted libraries

PRIVACY
No account. No sign-in. No analytics. No advertising ID. Books and progress stay on the device - we run no servers.

LANGUAGES
54 languages, following the system language, including complete Simplified and Traditional Chinese.

FREE SOFTWARE
AGPLv3. Source code: https://github.com/tt7247129-sys/omnishelf
