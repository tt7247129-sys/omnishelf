# Privacy Policy

Last updated: **9 November 2026**

There are two things called OmniShelf and they behave differently. The application on your
phone collects nothing. This website, like most websites, uses cookies, and may display
advertising served by third parties. This page describes both, plainly and in the order the
requirements usually ask for.

## 1. The Android application

The OmniShelf app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDK and no
crash-reporting SDK. Your library, reading progress, bookmarks and notes are stored in
local storage on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

The app uses the internet permission only for actions you start: opening an OPDS catalogue
you added, downloading a book, or fetching a cover. Those requests go from your device
directly to the server you chose; no server operated by this project is involved, and there
is none to send anything to.

The app requests broad file access so that it can index book files that already exist on
your device. It reads ebook and document formats and writes only to the book folder you
choose and to its own directory.

## 2. Cookies

This website uses cookies.

Cookies are small text files placed on your device that allow a site or a service to
recognise a browser across visits. They are used here for two purposes: keeping the site
working correctly, and — when advertising is enabled — allowing third-party advertising
systems to deliver and measure ads.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking third-party cookies
generally prevents personalised advertising from being delivered, though you will still see
advertising; it will simply be less relevant to you.

## 3. Third-party advertising, including Google AdSense

This website may use third-party advertising services, including **Google AdSense**, to
display advertising.

**Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior
visits to this website or to other websites.**

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based
on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. This is commonly described
as personalised or interest-based advertising.

These third-party systems operate under their own privacy policies. We do not receive, and
cannot read, the individual data those systems hold about you. We see aggregate reports —
how many pages were viewed, roughly which countries visitors came from, whether an ad slot
performed. Nothing in those reports identifies a person.

## 4. Other third-party ad networks and vendors

Beyond Google AdSense, other third-party vendors and advertising networks may also serve
ads on this site, now or in the future. Any such vendor may place or read cookies and
similar technologies on your browser in order to serve advertising based on your visits to
this and other websites.

Where an additional network is used, this policy will name it here before it is deployed,
together with the opt-out mechanism it offers. The general opt-out mechanisms in section 5
apply to most vendors, including those not named individually.

## 5. How to opt out of personalised advertising

You can opt out of personalised advertising in several ways:

- **Google Ads Settings** — [google.com/settings/ads](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).
  This is where you can turn off ad personalisation for your Google account, including ads
  served by Google on this site.
- **www.aboutads.info** — [aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
  A single place to opt out of interest-based advertising from many participating vendors
  in the United States.
- **Your Online Choices** — [youronlinechoices.com](https://www.youronlinechoices.com).
  The European equivalent, covering vendors operating in the EU and UK.
- **Your browser's own cookie controls.** Blocking third-party cookies generally prevents
  personalised advertising, though you will still see advertising.

Opting out of personalised advertising does not remove advertising. It stops advertising
from being tailored to your inferred interests.

## 6. Analytics and server logs

This site does not run a first-party analytics product that profiles individual visitors.
The hosting provider records standard request logs — IP address, user agent, requested path,
timestamp — for operational and security purposes. Those logs are retained for a short
period and are not combined with any other data.

## 7. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal
information from them. The application is rated for general audiences and collects no data
at all.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or
deletion of personal data held about you, and to object to processing. Because this site
holds no account data, most such requests concern data held by advertising vendors, which
you must address to them directly using the controls in section 5. If you believe this site
holds data about you, write to the address below and we will answer within 30 days.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If advertising is enabled on this site, if an additional advertising network is added, or if
the app begins to collect anything it does not collect today, this page will be updated
first and the date at the top will change.
