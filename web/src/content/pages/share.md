# Someone shared a quote with you

It was made with **OmniShelf**, a free ebook and document reader for Android.

## What it is

A reader for the books you already own. It opens 34 file types — PDF, EPUB, MOBI, AZW3,
FB2, DJVU, comic archives CBZ and CBR, DOCX, ODT, RTF, Markdown, plain text and more —
and it reads them offline.

There is no advertising, no in-app purchase, no subscription, no account and no tracking
code of any kind. The application contains no analytics SDK and does not read an
advertising ID. Your books, your highlights and your reading progress stay on your phone;
the project runs no servers.

## What it does well

- Text-to-speech with the device's own voice, working without a connection
- Day, night, sepia and custom themes, with fine control of type and spacing
- Highlights, notes and bookmarks, plus a continue-reading card on the shelf
- Five shelves, four layouts, instant search, OPDS catalogues
- 54 interface languages, following the language of your phone

## Get it

  closed testing while the store review period runs
- [APK from the releases page](https://github.com/tt7247129-sys/omnishelf/releases) —

## Make your own card

Long-press a word while reading, open the selection panel and choose **Share quote**.
Three card styles, no watermark over the text, no account needed.
