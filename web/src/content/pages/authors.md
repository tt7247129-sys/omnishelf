# The editor

Everything on this site is written by one person, publishing under the handle
**@tt7247129-sys** — the same account that owns the application's repository and answers
the mail at [hello@omnishelf.cc](mailto:hello@omnishelf.cc).

The handle is a pen name in the narrow sense: it is the identity the project has used since
its first commit, and it is deliberately not a legal name. Publishing a home address next
to a software project that anyone can download is not a trade worth making. What is
verifiable is the account, the commit history and the mailbox; if you need to reach a
person rather than a byline, the mailbox reaches one.

## Background

I read PDFs for a living before I wrote software for reading them: papers, standards,
scanned manuals, the kind of document that arrives as 300 pages of two-column text and gets
read on a phone because that is the device that is there. The complaints in these notes
started as my own — a citation that pointed at a page number the app had invented, a
highlight that vanished on an e-ink screen, a table of contents that took three taps to
reach the chapter I already knew I wanted.

Building the reader came later, and it changed the notes. It is easy to have opinions about
pagination when you have never implemented it; it is much harder to keep them once you have
spent a week on the question of what a page number means when the text reflows. Most of
what is published here is the residue of that: a position I held, a thing I measured, and
the version of the position that survived the measurement.

## What I am and am not

I am not a librarian, a publisher, or an academic. I do not have credentials in
bibliography, and where a note touches on copyright or lending it says so and stays
descriptive — it is not legal advice and it is not written by a lawyer.

What I do have is a working reader in front of me, the ability to test claims on real
files, and the habit of writing down what changed my mind. The notes are corrected when
they are wrong, in public, with the correction visible.
