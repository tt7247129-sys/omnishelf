**OmniShelf: a reader's guide** is written and owned by the project, so it is the one book on
this shelf the site hosts itself — an EPUB you can download and keep, without an account.

Eight chapters, each a few paragraphs: what the app is, pointing it at a folder, the three
reading settings worth changing, highlights and the quote card, listening offline, where books
come from, privacy in concrete terms, and what to send when something breaks.

Written in the same voice as the rest of this site: what the reader does, and why it does it
that way. Licensed CC BY 4.0 — copy it, quote it, put it on a shelf.
