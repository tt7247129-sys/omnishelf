OmniShelf

A free ebook and document reader for Android. No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking.

  • 34 file types — PDF, EPUB, MOBI, CBZ, CBR, DJVU, DOCX and more
  • Offline text-to-speech, highlights, notes and bookmarks
  • OPDS catalog support, library shelves and search
  • Free software under the AGPLv3
Not on Google Play yet. OmniShelf is an independent fork of the open-source Librera Reader project. It is not the official Librera app and is not affiliated with its author.
