Privacy Policy

App: OmniShelf  ·  Effective 7 November 2026  ·  Last updated 7 November 2026

OmniShelf collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no ads, no trackers, no crash reporting.

1. What we do not collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, including:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or account identifiers
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID)
  • Location
  • Contacts, SMS, or call logs
  • Your reading history, book titles, progress, bookmarks, or notes
  • Usage behaviour, crash logs, or performance telemetry

2. Where your data lives

Your library, reading progress, bookmarks, notes and settings are stored only in local storage on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them. We operate no servers and therefore cannot read, back up, or restore your data.

Cloud sync is not offered in this app. There is no sign-in and no way for your library to leave the device through the app.

3. Network access

The app uses the internet permission only for actions you initiate:

ScenarioDescription
OPDS catalogsThe app connects directly to a catalog address you add
Book downloadsDirect connection to the server hosting the file
Cover or metadata fetchDirect connection, on your explicit request

These requests go directly from your device to the target server, with no intermediary operated by us. Target sites may log your IP under their own privacy policies; that is outside our control.

4. Permissions

PermissionPurposeIf denied
Storage / all-files accessScan, open and organise ebook files already on your deviceCannot import local books
NetworkSee section 3Online catalogs unavailable
NotificationsPlayback controls, reading remindersReading unaffected
Foreground serviceKeep text-to-speech running while the screen is offTTS stops in background
BiometricOptional app lockApp lock unavailable

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or SMS access.

5. Third parties

  • Distribution: when installed through Google Play, Google processes install and diagnostic data under its own privacy policy.
  • Text-to-speech: uses the on-device system TTS engine; text is processed locally.
  • Bundled libraries: this build includes Google Play services libraries inherited from the upstream project. They belong to a cloud feature that is disabled here and are never invoked, so they do not transmit anything.
  • No other third-party data recipients.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no user data.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, GDPR / CCPA / PIPL rights of access, correction, deletion and portability are satisfied by design — your data never leaves your device.

8. Changes

If a future version introduces any data collection, this page will be updated with a new effective date and an in-app notice. Until then, the commitment above stands.

9. Contact

OmniShelf Project · hello@omnishelf.cc · source code

隐私政策

应用:OmniShelf · 生效日期 2026 年 11 月 7 日

OmniShelf 不收集你的任何数据。没有账号,没有统计,没有广告, 没有追踪,没有崩溃上报。

1. 我们不收集什么

本应用不收集、不上传、不存储任何个人信息,包括:姓名、邮箱、电话、账号;设备标识符 (IMEI、Android ID、广告 ID);位置信息;通讯录、短信、通话记录;你阅读的书名、进度、 书签、笔记;使用行为、崩溃日志、性能数据。

2. 数据存在哪里

书架、阅读进度、书签、笔记与设置,仅保存在你设备的本地存储中, 卸载即删除。我们没有服务器,因此无法读取、备份或恢复你的数据。

本应用不提供云同步。没有登录入口,你的书库无法通过本应用离开设备。

3. 网络访问

仅在以下由你主动触发的场景使用网络:在线书库(OPDS)、下载书籍、抓取封面或元数据。 这些请求由你的设备直接发往目标服务器,不经过我们的任何中间服务。 目标站点可能按其自身隐私政策记录你的 IP。

4. 权限说明

权限用途拒绝后
存储 / 所有文件访问扫描、打开、整理设备上已有电子书无法导入本地书籍
网络见第 3 节在线书库不可用
通知播放控制、阅读提醒不影响阅读
前台服务息屏后继续朗读后台朗读中断
生物识别可选的应用锁无法使用应用锁

本应用不申请位置、相机、麦克风、通讯录、短信权限。

5. 第三方

通过 Google Play 安装时,Google 按其隐私政策处理安装与诊断信息;朗读调用系统内置语音引擎, 文本在本地处理。本构建包含上游遗留的 Google Play services 库,属于一个已关闭的云功能, 从不被调用,不会传输任何数据。无其他第三方数据接收方。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童定向提供,且不收集任何用户数据。

7. 你的权利

由于我们不持有你的任何数据,GDPR / CCPA /《个人信息保护法》下的访问、更正、删除、 可携带权在设计上已天然满足——数据从未离开你的设备。

8. 政策变更

若未来版本引入任何数据收集行为,本页会更新生效日期并在应用内显著提示。
