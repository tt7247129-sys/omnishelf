## 三、完整说明(上限 4000 字符)

**英文**
```
OmniShelf reads your books. That is the whole product. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no trackers, and nothing locked behind an upgrade.

WHAT IT OPENS
34 file types, including:
- PDF and XPS
- EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, AZW4, FB2, PDB, PRC
- DJVU, and the comic archives CBZ and CBR
- DOCX (Android 8 and newer), DOC, ODT, RTF, Markdown
- TXT, HTML, XHTML, MHT, JSON, ZIP

READING
- Day, night, sepia and fully custom themes
- Fine control of font, margins, line spacing and alignment
- Single page, two page and scrolling modes
- Text-to-speech using your device's built-in voice: works offline
- Highlights, notes and bookmarks with quick navigation
- Auto-scroll, tap zones, volume-key page turns
- Continue reading: a card on the shelf takes you back to the last page you stopped at

SHARE CARDS
- Long-press a word to select it, then tap "Share quote card"
- Six layouts to choose from: date card, cover ticket, vertical spine, cover band, cinnabar frame, gallery
- Each card carries a cinnabar seal, a paper texture and a perforated ticket stub, so it looks printed
- The card adds the book, the author, the date and a one-line introduction
- A QR code points at omnishelf.cc today and at the download page once the app is public
- Your last chosen layout is remembered

LIBRARY
- Five shelves: recent, library, folders, favourites and bookmarks
- Grid, compact, list and cover layouts; sort by date, title, author, size
- Instant search across titles, authors and series
- Pin a book to the top of the shelf, long-press a cover to pin it
- OPDS catalogues: connect any public or self-hosted library
- Covers and metadata read directly from your files

PRIVACY
No account. No sign-in. No analytics. No advertising ID.
Your books, reading progress and notes stay in local storage on your device. We run no servers, so we could not read them even if we wanted to.
Network access is used only when you open an online catalogue or download a book. Those connections go straight from your device to that server, never through us.

LANGUAGES
The interface is available in 54 languages and follows your system language automatically, with Simplified and Traditional Chinese fully translated.

FREE SOFTWARE
OmniShelf is free and open source under the AGPLv3. The complete source for every release is public:
https://github.com/tt7247129-sys/omnishelf

ABOUT THIS PROJECT
OmniShelf is an independent fork of the open-source Librera Reader project. It is not the official Librera app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by its author. The licence they chose is what makes a project like this possible.
OmniShelf is a standalone project, not connected to or endorsed by any other company, product or service that happens to share the name.
"Free" here means free of charge and free as in freedom: you may use OmniShelf at work, at school, in a library or inside a business. The AGPL guarantees that. This is a community project, not a business.

A NOTE FROM THE DEVELOPER
This app is built by one person in his spare time. Bugs will happen - please be patient and tell me about them, and I will fix them as quickly as I can. If you find it useful, a good rating means a lot: it is the only thing that keeps this project going. Feedback: hello@omnishelf.cc
```
