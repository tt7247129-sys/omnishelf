# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**App:** OmniShelf
**Effective:** 2026-11-07
**Last updated:** 2026-11-07

---

## English

### The short version

**OmniShelf collects nothing.** No account, no analytics, no ads, no trackers,
no crash reporting.

### 1. What we do not collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, including:

- Name, email address, phone number, or account identifiers
- Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID)
- Location
- Contacts, SMS, or call logs
- Your reading history, book titles, progress, bookmarks, or notes
- Usage behaviour, crash logs, or performance telemetry

### 2. Where your data lives

Your library, reading progress, bookmarks, notes and settings are stored
**only in local storage on your device**. Uninstalling the app removes them.
We operate no servers and therefore cannot read, back up, or restore your data.

Cloud sync is **not** offered in this build. There is no way to sign in and
no way for your library to leave the device through the app.

### 3. Network access

The app uses the internet permission only for actions you initiate:

| Scenario | Description |
|---|---|
| OPDS catalogs | The app connects directly to a catalog address you add |
| Book downloads | Direct connection to the server hosting the file |
| Cover or metadata fetch | Direct connection, on your explicit request |

These requests go **directly from your device to the target server**, with no
intermediary operated by us. Target sites may log your IP under their own
privacy policies; that is outside our control.

### 4. Permissions

| Permission | Purpose | If denied |
|---|---|---|
| Storage / all-files access | Scan, open and organise ebook files already on your device | Cannot import local books |
| Network | See section 3 | Online catalogs unavailable |
| Notifications | Playback controls, reading reminders | Reading unaffected |
| Foreground service | Keep text-to-speech running while the screen is off | TTS stops in background |
| Biometric | Optional app lock | App lock unavailable |

The app does **not** request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or SMS access.

### 5. Third parties

- **Distribution:** when installed through Google Play, Google processes install
  and diagnostic data under its own privacy policy.
- **Text-to-speech:** uses the on-device system TTS engine; text is processed locally.
- **Bundled libraries:** this build includes Google Play services libraries inherited
  from the upstream project. They belong to a cloud feature that is disabled here and
  are never invoked, so they do not transmit anything.
- No other third-party data recipients.

### 6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no user data.

### 7. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, GDPR / CCPA / PIPL rights of access, correction,
deletion and portability are satisfied by design — your data never leaves your device.

### 8. Changes

If a future version introduces any data collection, this page will be updated with a
new effective date and an in-app notice. Until then, the commitment above stands.

- Email: hello@omnishelf.cc

## 中文

### 一句话

**OmniShelf 不收集你的任何数据。** 没有账号,没有统计,没有广告,没有追踪,没有崩溃上报。

### 1. 我们不收集什么

本应用不收集、不上传、不存储任何个人信息,包括:

- 姓名、邮箱、电话、账号
- 设备标识符(IMEI、Android ID、广告 ID)
- 位置信息
- 通讯录、短信、通话记录
- 你阅读的书名、进度、书签、笔记
- 使用行为、崩溃日志、性能数据

### 2. 数据存在哪里

书架、阅读进度、书签、笔记与设置,**仅保存在你设备的本地存储中**。卸载即删除。
我们没有服务器,因此无法读取、备份或恢复你的数据。

本版本**不提供云同步**。没有登录入口,你的书库无法通过本应用离开设备。

### 3. 网络访问

仅在以下由你主动触发的场景使用网络:

| 场景 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| 在线书库(OPDS) | 直连你添加的目录地址 |
| 下载书籍 | 直连书籍所在服务器 |
| 封面/元数据抓取 | 在你明确请求时直连 |

这些请求**由你的设备直接发往目标服务器**,不经过我们的任何中间服务。
目标站点可能按其自身隐私政策记录你的 IP,这不在我们控制范围内。

### 4. 权限说明

| 权限 | 用途 | 拒绝后 |
|---|---|---|
| 存储 / 所有文件访问 | 扫描、打开、整理设备上已有电子书 | 无法导入本地书籍 |
| 网络 | 见第 3 节 | 在线书库不可用 |
| 通知 | 播放控制、阅读提醒 | 不影响阅读 |
| 前台服务 | 息屏后继续朗读 | 后台朗读中断 |
| 生物识别 | 可选的应用锁 | 无法使用应用锁 |

本应用**不申请**位置、相机、麦克风、通讯录、短信权限。

### 5. 第三方

- **分发渠道**:通过 Google Play 安装时,Google 按其隐私政策处理安装与诊断信息。
- **朗读**:调用系统内置语音引擎,文本在本地处理。
- **内置库**:本构建包含上游遗留的 Google Play services 库,属于一个在此版本中已关闭的
  云功能,从不被调用,因此不会传输任何数据。
- 无其他第三方数据接收方。

### 6. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童定向提供,且不收集任何用户数据。

### 7. 你的权利

由于我们不持有你的任何数据,GDPR / CCPA /《个人信息保护法》下的访问、更正、删除、
可携带权在设计上已天然满足——数据从未离开你的设备。

### 8. 政策变更

若未来版本引入任何数据收集行为,本页会更新生效日期并在应用内显著提示。
在那种情况发生前,上述承诺持续有效。
